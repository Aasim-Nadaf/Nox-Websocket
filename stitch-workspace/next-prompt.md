A minimalist new message / compose screen for a messaging app.

**DESIGN SYSTEM (REQUIRED):**

- **Palette**: Strictly stark white, solid blacks, and light grays.
- **Geometry**: Extreme border-radii (fully rounded pills) on all components.
- **Typography**: Clean, bold sans-serif.
- **Elements**: Use solid black pill shapes for primary actions/messages, and light gray pill shapes for inputs/search bars. No sharp corners. Floating components preferred over full-width bars.

**Page Structure:**

1. Header: Stark white background, a bold black "New Message" title on the left, and a "Cancel" text button on the right in medium gray.
2. "To" Field: A highly rounded light gray pill-shaped input area. It contains the text "To: " followed by a softly blinking cursor or empty placeholder.
3. Suggested Contacts List: Below the "To" field, a section titled "Suggested" in small medium gray uppercase font. A vertical list of 3-4 contacts. Each contact has a circular avatar, bold black name, and a solid black circular "+" button on the far right. Use generous white space between items. No visual dividers.
4. Bottom Area: Empty white space, giving a feeling of airiness, with a solid black floating action button (FAB) containing a white dialpad icon in the bottom right corner.
